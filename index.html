Drag in one or more inverter Excel reports from Sunsynk Connect, a single day or a whole month, and see where your power came from, what you paid and what you saved.

or
  1. In Sunsynk Connect, open Equipment → Inverter and download the report (Excel) for the dates you want.

  3. Set your price per kWh under Rates & costs.
Your file is read in this browser only. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

    Rates & costs

    Saved in this browser for next time.

    R/ kWh
    What you pay the municipality or Eskom per kWh, incl. VAT.
    R/ kWh
    What you're paid for power sent to the grid. Leave at 0 if you don't sell back.
    R/ month
    Service and network fees you pay no matter what. Used in the monthly outlook.
    R
    Optional. Used to estimate payback time.
    Latitude, longitude for the weather backdrop. Leave blank to use your plant's location (or Johannesburg). Rounded to ~1 km before it's sent to Open-Meteo.
    kWp
    Total panel size, used to estimate how much solar was available. E.g. 16 × 545 W + 10 × 430 W ≈ 13 kWp.
    kWh
    Used for "full in…" and "lasts until…" estimates.
    %
    Your Use Timer SOC. Used to spot when the battery ran out.

    Connect Sunsynk

    Pull data live from your Sunsynk Connect account. It refreshes every 5 minutes while this page is open.

    Your login goes straight from this browser to Sunsynk (api.sunsynk.net). It isn't sent anywhere else, and the password is never saved. "Stay signed in" keeps only the session token on this device.

    This uses Sunsynk's unofficial web API. It may stop working if Sunsynk changes it.
